## Executive Summary

The playbook defines the shortest useful operating path for the validated
workspace. It keeps the canonical spine authoritative and turns the rest of
the surfaces into bounded, repeatable derivations.

The practical value is simple:

- recovery stays predictable;
- workflows stay short and repeatable;
- compaction stays safe;
- promotion stays reversible;
- authority stays singular.

## Purpose

The playbook exists to make the validated workflow easy to repeat without
turning it into a second system of truth.

It tells the maintainer what to read, what to keep stable, and what to reject.

## Canonical Spine

The canonical spine remains the base for all reuse:

- the ULTIMA mission report;
- `tasks/last_session.json`;
- `tasks/last_session.md`;
- `rules/MEMORY_GOVERNANCE.md`;
- Vortex as navigation and promotion only.

## Operating Chain

The playbook keeps the operating path split into controlled stages:

1. Lot 0: read-only audit and baseline.
2. Lot 1: minimal context package.
3. Lot 2: measured pilot work.
4. Lot 3: direct MCP on known targets with structured discovery as fallback.
5. Lot 4: hardening and controlled readiness.
6. Phase 5: repeatable workflows.
7. Phase 6: compaction control.

## Operating Rules

1. Keep canonical evidence first.
2. Keep derived views regenerable.
3. Keep human validation at transitions.
4. Keep hooks inert unless explicitly validated.
5. Keep fuzzy search secondary.
6. Keep promotion reversible and traceable.
7. Keep compaction safe and non-authoritative.

## Repository Health

The playbook is healthy when:

- the spine is current;
- the workflow remains easy to recover;
- the derived surfaces stay aligned with the canon;
- closure can be repeated without guesswork;
- no hidden authority layer appears.

## Documentation Quality Gates

- Headings are complete and in order.
- The workflow is readable without the full mission archive.

- Limitations are explicit.
- The guide stays short enough to use.

## Limitations

- This is a working guide, not a full product spec.
- It depends on the validated local canon.
- It does not replace repository-specific policy files.
- It stays meaningful only while the canonical spine stays stable.

## Final Verdict

The Playbook Final is the compact operational layer for the validated
Context Engineering model.

It keeps the canon singular, the workflows repeatable, and the final
authority human.
